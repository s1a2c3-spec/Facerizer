Delivery Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Facerizer is a digital service. We do not sell or ship any physical products, so there is no shipping, no delivery charge and no delivery time to wait for.

How you get access

  • Free plan: available as soon as you create an account and confirm you are 18 or older.
  • Paid plans (Pro and Premium): your plan is switched on in your account right after your payment is confirmed. This is normally within a few minutes.
  • Your reports: each analysis usually takes under a minute, and the report appears in your dashboard.

If something goes wrong

  • If you paid and your plan does not show within 30 minutes, email us from the address on your account with the date and amount of the payment. We will fix it or refund you, within 2 business days.
  • If an analysis fails because of a problem on our side, it does not use up your plan limit.

Where we deliver

The service is available online and can be used from anywhere with an internet connection. Prices are shown in Indian rupees. For refunds, see our Refund Policy.
