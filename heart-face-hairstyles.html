Hairstyles, Glasses and Beard Styles for a Heart Face

Updated 2026-10-21

A heart-shaped face is widest across the forehead and cheekbones, and it narrows to a smaller, often pointed chin.

The goal with a heart face is to balance a wider upper face with more fullness lower down. Use the ideas below as a starting point, and remember that every face shape looks good with the right cut. Your hair type and your own taste matter more than any rule.

How to tell if you have a heart face

  • Your forehead is the widest part of your face.
  • Your cheekbones are high and your jaw is narrow.
  • Your chin is smaller or more pointed.

Look straight into a mirror with your hair pulled back, or use a clear front-facing photo. Compare the width of your forehead, cheekbones and jaw, and the length from hairline to chin. See how to measure your face shape, or let Facerizer measure it from your photo.

Hairstyles for a heart face

Worth styling with care: Very tall volume on top and slicked-back styles that show the whole forehead can emphasize the width of the upper face. Choose them only if you like the look.

Glasses for a heart face

  • Often work well: Frames that are heavier at the bottom, and round or oval frames.
  • Also worth trying: Aviators suit a heart shape well.
  • Keep in mind: Top-heavy browline frames can add to the width at the top.

Beard and facial hair for a heart face

  • A fuller beard adds width at the jaw and chin.
  • Keep it neat. A very sharp, pointed chin beard adds to the narrow look.

Facial hair is optional. A clean-shaven look suits every face shape, and you should wear whatever you are comfortable with.

Quick tips

  • Add width low, near the jaw.
  • Soften the forehead with a swept fringe.
  • Light or thin frames at the top look balanced.

Common questions about heart faces

What hairstyle suits a heart-shaped face?

Chin-length bobs, layers that start near the chin, side-swept fringes and waves below the ears balance a wider forehead and a narrower chin.

What glasses suit a heart-shaped face?

Frames that are a little heavier at the bottom, such as round, oval and aviator, balance the wider top of the face.

Should I grow a beard with a heart-shaped face?

A fuller beard can add width at the jaw and chin and balance the shape. It is your choice, and a clean-shaven look works too.
