Hairstyles, Glasses and Beard Styles for a Round Face

Updated 2026-10-21

A round face is about as wide as it is long. It has full cheeks, a softly rounded jaw and a rounded chin, with few sharp angles.

The goal with a round face is to add height and a little length, and bring in some angles. Use the ideas below as a starting point, and remember that every face shape looks good with the right cut. Your hair type and your own taste matter more than any rule.

How to tell if you have a round face

  • Your face length and cheekbone width are almost the same.
  • The widest part of your face is across the cheeks.
  • Your jaw and chin are soft and rounded.

Look straight into a mirror with your hair pulled back, or use a clear front-facing photo. Compare the width of your forehead, cheekbones and jaw, and the length from hairline to chin. See how to measure your face shape, or let Facerizer measure it from your photo.

Hairstyles for a round face

Worth styling with care: Very full volume at the sides, a blunt chin-length bob that ends at the widest point, and a thick straight fringe can add width. Choose these only if you like how they look.

Glasses for a round face

  • Often work well: Rectangle, square and angular frames, and browline frames.
  • Also worth trying: Frames that are slightly wider than the face.
  • Keep in mind: Small round frames can echo the curve of a round face, so try them only if you like the look.

Beard and facial hair for a round face

  • A neat beard with a defined line along the jaw adds structure.
  • Keep the sides short and allow a little length at the chin. A very full, round beard can add width.

Facial hair is optional. A clean-shaven look suits every face shape, and you should wear whatever you are comfortable with.

Quick tips

  • Think height and angles: a part, a swept fringe or volume on top.
  • Keep side volume light.
  • Frames wider than your face help balance round lines.

Common questions about round faces

What hairstyle suits a round face?

Styles that add height and a little length, such as a quiff, a side part with volume, long layers or a side-swept pixie, work well on a round face.

Should I avoid short hair with a round face?

No. Short hair can look great. Choose short sides with some volume on top rather than a rounded shape that follows the face.

What glasses suit a round face?

Rectangle, square and angular frames add definition. Browline frames also work. Pick a frame slightly wider than your face.
