Hairstyles, Glasses and Beard Styles for a Square Face

Updated 2026-10-21

A square face has a forehead, cheekbones and jaw of similar width, with a strong, angular jawline and a fairly short length.

The goal with a square face is to either soften the angles with texture and curves, or show off the strong jaw. Use the ideas below as a starting point, and remember that every face shape looks good with the right cut. Your hair type and your own taste matter more than any rule.

How to tell if you have a square face

  • Your forehead, cheekbones and jaw are close in width.
  • Your jawline is strong and angular.
  • Your face length is only a little more than its width.

Look straight into a mirror with your hair pulled back, or use a clear front-facing photo. Compare the width of your forehead, cheekbones and jaw, and the length from hairline to chin. See how to measure your face shape, or let Facerizer measure it from your photo.

Hairstyles for a square face

Worth styling with care: Very blunt, straight cuts that end at the jaw can echo the square lines, and sharp geometric styles can add more angles. If you like a strong look, they can still work for you.

Glasses for a square face

  • Often work well: Round, oval and aviator frames soften the angles.
  • Also worth trying: Slightly curved rectangles are a good middle option.
  • Keep in mind: Boxy square frames repeat the angles, so use them if you like a bold look.

Beard and facial hair for a square face

  • Light stubble or a short beard shows the jaw.
  • A very square, thick beard adds more angles, so keep the edges soft if you want a gentler look.

Facial hair is optional. A clean-shaven look suits every face shape, and you should wear whatever you are comfortable with.

Quick tips

  • Texture and waves work well against sharp lines.
  • If you like your jaw, keep sides short and neat.
  • Choose frames with some curve.

Common questions about square faces

What hairstyle suits a square face?

Soft layers, waves, side-swept fringes and textured crops all work well. They soften the angles or let the strong jaw show, depending on your taste.

How do I soften a square jaw?

Add texture, waves or a side-swept fringe, and pick rounder glasses. These create curves that balance the angles.

Do short haircuts suit a square face?

Yes. A short textured crop with a clean finish highlights a strong jawline.
