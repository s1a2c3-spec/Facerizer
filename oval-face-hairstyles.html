Hairstyles, Glasses and Beard Styles for an Oval Face

Updated 2026-10-21

An oval face is about one and a half times as long as it is wide. The forehead is slightly wider than the chin, and the jaw curves softly to a rounded chin. It is often called the most balanced shape because no single area stands out.

The goal with an oval face is to keep the natural balance and avoid styles that hide it. Use the ideas below as a starting point, and remember that every face shape looks good with the right cut. Your hair type and your own taste matter more than any rule.

How to tell if you have an oval face

  • Your face is roughly 1.5 times as long as it is wide at the cheekbones.
  • Your forehead is a little wider than your jaw.
  • Your jaw curves gently into a rounded chin, with no sharp corners.

Look straight into a mirror with your hair pulled back, or use a clear front-facing photo. Compare the width of your forehead, cheekbones and jaw, and the length from hairline to chin. See how to measure your face shape, or let Facerizer measure it from your photo.

Hairstyles for an oval face

Worth styling with care: A very heavy fringe that covers most of the forehead can hide the balance, and extremely tall styles can make the face look longer. Neither is wrong, so treat them as choices rather than rules.

Glasses for an oval face

  • Often work well: Rectangle, square, aviator and browline frames.
  • Also worth trying: Round frames for a softer look.
  • Keep in mind: Keep the frame about as wide as the widest part of your face.

Beard and facial hair for an oval face

  • Short beard, light stubble or a neat moustache all work.
  • Keep the beard even so it does not stretch the face, and go easy on very long, pointed styles.

Facial hair is optional. A clean-shaven look suits every face shape, and you should wear whatever you are comfortable with.

Quick tips

  • Because most styles suit you, start with what you like and what fits your hair type.
  • If you wear a fringe, keep it light so your forehead still shows a little.
  • Check that glasses are not much wider than your face.

Common questions about oval faces

What hairstyle suits an oval face best?

Most hairstyles suit an oval face. Layered cuts, side parts, textured crops and moderate quiffs are popular because they keep the natural balance.

Is an oval face the best face shape?

No. Every face shape works well with the right styling. Oval is simply balanced, which gives you more choices, but a good haircut and glasses can suit any shape.

Can I wear a fringe with an oval face?

Yes. A light or side-swept fringe works well. A very heavy, straight fringe can hide the forehead, so choose a softer one if you want to keep it open.
