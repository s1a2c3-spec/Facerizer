What changed

Differences under 4 points usually come from light, angle or expression, so treat them as no clear change. Facerizer looks at grooming, styling and presentation, and never says your face has improved or got worse.

For a fair comparison

  • Use the same light, ideally natural light facing a window.
  • Look straight at the camera with a relaxed expression.
  • Keep the camera at eye level and the same distance.
  • Take the photo at a similar time of day.
