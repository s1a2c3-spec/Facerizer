About Facerizer

Facerizer is an AI grooming and style coach. You upload a clear photo of your face, and it finds your face shape, describes your visible features, and suggests hairstyles, glasses, grooming habits and a step-by-step plan. It is built to help you pick what suits you, not to judge how you look.

Who runs Facerizer

Facerizer is run by Sachin Patel, an individual proprietor based in Pratapgarh, Uttar Pradesh, India. It is a small independent project, and you can write to the person who builds it at the email address below.

Why we built it

Choosing a haircut, a pair of glasses or a beard style can feel like guesswork, and it is hard to explain to a barber what you want. Facerizer gives you clear options based on your face shape and your own preferences, with the exact words to tell your barber, so you can make confident choices.

How it works

  1. Upload a photo. We check the quality on your own device first.
  2. We measure and read your visible features. Facial landmarks give measured proportions, and AI turns them into plain-language suggestions.
  3. Get your report. Face shape, strengths, hairstyle ideas, glasses, grooming tips and, on paid plans, a feature-by-feature report and a 30-day plan.
  4. Follow your plan. Tick off tasks, keep a streak and track your progress over time.

You can see exactly what a report looks like in our example report, or learn about face shapes in our face shapes guide.

What we believe

  • It is not a beauty rating. The score is a subjective estimate based on one photo, and you can hide it at any time.
  • It is not medical advice. Skin notes are general and cosmetic only.
  • Your photo is yours. Photos are private, never sold or shared for advertising, and you can delete them or your whole account at any time. Read our Privacy Policy.
  • For adults only. Facerizer is for people aged 18 and over.
