Questions

What is Facerizer?

Facerizer is an AI grooming and style coach. It analyzes a photo of your face to find your face shape and visible features, then suggests hairstyles, beard styles, glasses and a personalized improvement plan.

How does AI face shape detection work?

The photo is checked for quality, then facial landmarks are used to measure proportions such as forehead, mid-face, lower face and width. These measurements are used to classify your face shape (oval, round, square, rectangle, heart, diamond or triangle), and AI explains what suits it.

Is Facerizer a beauty rating?

No. The AI Appearance Analysis Score reflects visible facial characteristics in one photo. It is a subjective estimate, not a measure of your worth or attractiveness. You can hide scores and see only recommendations.

Is it free?

Yes, you can run one free analysis. Pro, with full reports and plans, starts at ₹399 a month. Prices include GST.

What happens to my photo?

Your photo is stored privately and shown only to you. We never sell or share face photos, and we do not use them to train AI unless you choose to opt in. You can delete a photo or your whole account at any time.

Can it tell me about my skin health?

No. Skin notes describe only what is visible and are cosmetic. General cosmetic guidance only. Consult a qualified dermatologist for persistent or concerning skin issues.

Who can use it?

Adults aged 18 and over.

Will I get the same result every time?

Measurements come from facial landmarks, so the same photo gives the same numbers. Lighting, camera angle and expression change the result, so use a clear, front-facing photo.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel your plan from your account. Refund details will be on the Refund Policy page.
