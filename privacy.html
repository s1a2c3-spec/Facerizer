Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Facerizer is an AI grooming and style coach. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We have written it in plain language. If something is unclear, please contact us.

1. Who we are

Facerizer is operated by Sachin Patel, an individual proprietor based in Pratapgarh, Uttar Pradesh, India. In this policy, "we", "us" and "Facerizer" mean the operator of this website. Contact details are in section 12.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, your name (if you give one), and how you signed in (email and password, or Google). We never see your Google password.
  • Profile choices (optional): age range, preferred hair length, facial hair preference, style preference, and whether you want to hide scores.
  • Consent records: the time you confirmed you are 18 or older and agreed to our Terms and this policy.
  • Face photos: only the photos you choose to save for analysis (see section 3).
  • Analysis results: when the analysis feature is available, the reports we create from your photos.
  • Payments: when paid plans are available, payments are handled by our payment provider. We receive the payment status and plan, not your card or UPI details.
  • Technical data: basic server logs kept by our hosting providers, and small items stored in your browser to keep you logged in and remember your light or dark theme.

3. Your face photos

Face photos can be sensitive, so we handle them with extra care:

  • Photos are checked on your own device before anything is uploaded. A photo is saved only if you tap "Save this photo".
  • Saved photos are stored in a private location. Only you can view them. They are not public.
  • We use your photo only to give you your analysis and recommendations. We do not use it to identify you or to match you with anyone.
  • We do not sell your photos, share them for advertising, or show them to other users.
  • We do not use your photos to train AI models. We select AI services and settings so that your photos are not used for model training.
  • You can delete any photo, or all of them, from your dashboard at any time.

4. How we use your data

  • To create and run your account and keep it secure.
  • To check your photo quality, create your analysis and personalize recommendations.
  • To apply plan limits and process payments when paid plans are available.
  • To respond to your messages and requests.
  • To prevent misuse, such as creating many accounts to avoid limits.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We use your data only for these purposes and only with your consent or another lawful basis under applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We use trusted service providers who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase: database, login and private photo storage.
  • Vercel: website hosting and server functions.
  • Google (Gemini) or Anthropic (Claude): the AI service we use at the time to read your photo and write your analysis, only when you request an analysis.
  • Google: only if you choose "Continue with Google" to sign in.
  • Razorpay: payments, when paid plans are available.

These providers may store or process data on servers outside India. We may also disclose data if required by law or a valid legal order.

6. How long we keep data

  • Photos stay until you delete them, or until you delete your account.
  • If you delete your account, we delete your profile and your saved photos. Deletion starts immediately. Copies in routine provider backups are removed as those backups expire.
  • We may keep limited records, such as payment records, for as long as the law requires.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a summary of the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct or update your data (you can edit your profile in your dashboard);
  • erase your data (you can delete photos and your account yourself in your dashboard);
  • withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing consent may mean we can no longer provide the service;
  • address a complaint about how we handle your data.

Write to us at the email in section 12 and we will reply within a reasonable time, usually within 7 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is operational.

8. Age

Facerizer is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has used the service, please tell us and we will delete the account.

9. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), access rules that let each person reach only their own data, and private storage for photos. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your login private. If a data breach affects you, we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

10. Cookies and similar technologies

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. We use small items in your browser only to keep you signed in and to remember your theme. If we add analytics later, we will update this policy.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Facerizer grows. We will change the "last updated" date above, and tell you about important changes by email or on the website.
